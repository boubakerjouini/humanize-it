## 📖 DESCRIPTION LONGUE (Full Narrative)

### The Problem

Every day, hundreds of millions of people use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to draft essays, emails, cover letters, and articles.

The result? An internet full of text that sounds... robotic. Predictable. Detectable.

Detection tools are now everywhere:
- **Turnitin** flags 70%+ of unmodified AI text — students risk academic penalties
- **GPTZero** is used by hundreds of universities and publishers
- **Recruiters** spot AI-written cover letters instantly — and discard them
- **Google** penalizes AI-generated content that doesn't add human value

So people are stuck. They either:
1. **Rewrite everything manually** — takes hours
2. **Use complex prompt tricks** — unreliable, still gets flagged
3. **Pay $20-30/month** for black-box tools that paraphrase blindly and often destroy the original meaning

There's no tool that's affordable, transparent, and actually works consistently.

---

### The Solution: HumanizeIt

HumanizeIt is not a paraphraser. It's a **pattern-aware style editor** that understands exactly why AI text gets detected — and fixes only what needs fixing.

**How it works:**

1. **Paste your text** — The engine analyzes it in < 500ms, entirely server-side, never sent to third-party APIs
2. **Get a Score** — A 0-100 AI probability score built on 24 specific pattern detectors + three statistical measures (burstiness, type-token ratio, Flesch readability)
3. **See the heatmap** — Every suspicious word, phrase, and sentence structure is highlighted and explained
4. **One-click humanize** — The rewriter targets only the flagged patterns, preserving your original tone and meaning
5. **Watch the score drop** — From 78 → 12. Before/after. Measurable.

---

### What Makes Us Different

**🔍 Transparency, not a black box**

Every other humanizer gives you a rewritten text and calls it done. We tell you *why* each element was flagged:

- *"'Delve' is Tier 1 AI vocabulary — used 847× more often by AI than humans"*
- *"3 consecutive sentences of identical length — burstiness score too low"*
- *"'It is worth noting' is a filler phrase absent in human writing"*

Users learn. They improve. That's retention.

**💰 Price that converts**

$9/month vs. $19-30 for direct competitors. For a student, that's less than a coffee per week. The purchase decision is nearly impulsive.

**⚡ Speed that doesn't compromise privacy**

Analysis runs in < 500ms. The text never leaves to a third-party API for scoring — only the rewrite step uses an LLM. This matters for professionals who handle confidential content.

## ❓ QUESTIONS ANTICIPÉES (avec réponses)

### Q1 — "Is this ethical? You're helping students cheat."

**Réponse :**
> There are two separate questions here: *is using AI to assist with writing wrong?* and *is detecting AI writing a reliable measure of academic integrity?*

> We don't take a position on the first — that's between students and their institutions. On the second: AI detection tools have a documented false positive rate of 15-30%. Native non-English speakers are flagged at disproportionate rates. Turnitin's own documentation acknowledges this.

> HumanizeIt serves ghostwriters, content teams, and job seekers just as much as students. The tool is neutral — like spell-check. We don't decide how it's used.

---

### Q2 — "How is this different from Quillbot or Undetectable.ai?"

**Réponse :**
> Three differences:

> **1. Transparency** — Every other tool is a black box. We show you exactly which patterns triggered detection and why. You see the heatmap. You understand the score.

> **2. Targeted rewriting** — We don't paraphrase your entire text (which usually destroys tone). We fix only the flagged patterns. Your writing structure stays intact.

> **3. Price** — $9/mo vs. $19-30 for direct competitors. No "usage credits" that expire. Flat monthly.

---

### Q3 — "What are the 24 patterns exactly?"

**Réponse :**
> They fall into 5 categories:

> - **Content patterns** (5): Hedging language, false balance, generic examples, missing personal anecdotes, lack of specificity
> - **Language patterns** (6): AI vocabulary tiers (500+ terms), zero contractions, passive voice overuse, filler phrases, transition addiction
> - **Style patterns** (5): Sentence length uniformity (burstiness), parallel structure overuse, zero sentence fragments, perfect punctuation, no colloquialisms
> - **Communication patterns** (4): No direct address, no rhetorical questions, no humor/irony, no em-dash usage
> - **Filler patterns** (4): "It is important to note", "In conclusion", "Furthermore", empty qualifiers

> We'll be publishing the full technical breakdown as a blog post — link in bio.

---

### Q4 — "Does it work on languages other than English?"

**Réponse :**
> Right now, English only — and that's intentional. The 24 patterns are calibrated specifically for English AI writing. Applying them to French or Spanish without recalibration would produce bad results.

> Multi-language support (French, Spanish, German) is on the roadmap for Month 2-3. We'll recalibrate the pattern detectors for each language separately. No shortcuts.

---

### Q5 — "Does my text get stored? Who sees it?"

**Réponse :**
> Free users: texts are analyzed and discarded. No storage.

> Pro users: texts are saved in your 30-day history (you can delete anytime).

> The **analysis phase** (scoring, pattern detection) never calls a third-party API — it runs entirely on our servers. Only the rewrite step uses GPT-4o-mini, and we send only the flagged segments, not your full text.

> We don't sell data. We don't train models on your content.

---

### Q6 — "What's the accuracy rate? Can it be fooled?"

**Réponse :**
> Our internal testing shows the score correlates strongly with GPTZero and Originality.ai results (r > 0.85). It's not identical — they use different models — but directionally very accurate.

> Can it be fooled? If you write 100% original human text, you should score < 15. If you write pure GPT output without modification, you should score > 70.

> The rewriter typically drops scores by 40-60 points in one pass. A second pass usually brings it below 20.

> We never claim 100% bypass guarantee — any tool that does is lying to you.

---

### Q7 — "Why build this vs. just using a better prompt?"

**Réponse :**
> We built exactly this for Month 1. Here's what we found:

> The best prompts ("write like a tired college student", "vary your sentence length", "avoid academic transitions") work about 40-60% of the time. They're inconsistent because the LLM still has its own patterns baked in.

> HumanizeIt is deterministic — it detects the same patterns every time, and the rewriter is specifically tuned to eliminate them. That's the difference between reliable and "usually works".

---

### Q8 — "Is the Free tier actually useful or just a demo?"

**Réponse :**
> 500 words/day is a real use case — a cover letter, a short essay introduction, a LinkedIn post. It's not a "taste and bait" tier.

> We deliberately made Free meaningful because we believe in letting the product sell itself. If you use HumanizeIt on 3 documents and it works, you'll upgrade. We don't need artificial limits to force that.

> The upgrade trigger is naturally volume — when you have more than a short document to humanize, $9/month is obvious.

> If you're a developer who wants early API access, DM me — happy to give you extended beta access in exchange for feedback.
